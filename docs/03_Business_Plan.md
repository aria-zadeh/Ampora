# Ampora — Business Plan

## 1. One-liner
A best-in-class auto-scheduler (everything FlowSavvy does) that also schedules the real you, makes starting free, and makes slacking cost you access to your own apps right now.

## 2. The problem
People do not procrastinate because they lack a schedule. They procrastinate because they cannot start, they plan for an aspirational self that never shows, and slacking is free in the moment while its cost is in the future. FlowSavvy and every AI calendar optimize time. None move behavior. You can have a perfect schedule and still scroll for three hours.

## 3. The solution
Full auto-scheduling parity (auto-schedule tasks around fixed events, split large tasks, balance workload, recurring tasks, priorities, dependencies, 1-click recalculate, calendar sync) plus three differentiators: a learning engine that plans around real behavior (Revealed Self, Focus DNA, energy/state), an initiation layer (starter action + Blindfold), and the headline: lock your own apps behind your task. Recovery Mode rebuilds the week without guilt. Full detail in the PRD.

## 4. Competitive landscape
- **AI calendars (FlowSavvy, Motion, Reclaim):** schedule your intentions; slacking stays free. FlowSavvy is the benchmark for quality and the closest competitor. It is beloved, polished, solo-built, and recently shipped a public API and MCP server.
- **Task managers (Todoist, TickTick, Notion):** organize tasks; the list is the wall, not the cure.
- **Focus/blockers (Opal, Forest, Freedom, One Sec):** block distractions, but disconnected from your actual schedule and tasks.
- **ADHD apps (Tiimo, Goblin.tools, Inflow):** point tools, often still framing the brain as broken.

**The empty middle Ampora owns:** the only product combining a real FlowSavvy-grade scheduler, behavioral learning, initiation, and work-aware app-locking (the lock is tied to the specific task, not a blunt timer). Positioning: *the scheduler that works with your brain instead of against it.*

## 5. Why this can win against a beloved incumbent
- **Different job:** FlowSavvy schedules time; Ampora moves behavior. Not a feature war, a category shift.
- **A hook that sells itself:** "an app that locks your Instagram until your homework is done" needs zero explanation, which is what makes student apps spread.
- **A moat a single feature cannot be:** the behavioral model (a schedule trained on one person's real habits) and the brand/identity are not copyable by cloning the lock button. FlowSavvy can copy a feature; it cannot copy a model of you or a worldview people identify with.

## 6. Market
Tens of millions of US students; ADHD diagnoses among teens and young adults have risen for a decade, and the far larger "I only work under pressure" group lives the same problem. This audience is online, shares tools that feel built for them, and responds to identity over feature lists. Beachhead: the founder's own school and clubs, then ADHD/study creator communities.

## 7. Monetization
Paid app, no free tier. Every user starts a 2-week free trial, then pays a monthly or annual subscription (annual is about 10% cheaper on a per-month basis). On iOS this runs through Apple In-App Purchase with an introductory free trial. The subscription covers the AI costs (the company pays per-call), so heavy AI like vision verification, the project chat, and grounded breakdowns are included rather than bring-your-own-key gated. A bring-your-own model key remains an optional power feature, not a requirement.

Implications: the growth motion (the lock-mechanic clip) drives trial signups rather than free installs, so the funnel is install, feel the aha in the trial, convert. Watch AI cost per active user against the subscription price to protect margin (the heaviest costs are vision verification and the project chat). No ads, no ad SDKs, which keeps the "we are on your side" brand and is required for a behavioral app used by minors.

## 8. Operations & legal reality (founder is a minor)
- **Store/payments accounts** require an adult or registered org. Path: a parent/guardian as legal account holder, or an LLC (some states allow a minor member with an adult organizer). Decide before submission.
- **Blocking-app review:** apps using Family Controls get extra App Store scrutiny. The panic valve, clear user control, and "the user restricts their own device" framing are what pass review. Build them from day one. Study how Opal/Brick are positioned.
- **Age floor 13** to stay out of COPPA. Enforce at signup. No under-13 targeting.
- **Data on minors:** behavioral and screen data is sensitive. Minimize, keep local where possible, no selling, no ad networks. Ethics and a trust/marketing asset.
- **No medical claims.** A productivity and focus tool, not an ADHD treatment or medical device.

## 9. Risks & mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Lock feels punitive, ADHD users churn | Consensual "lock your own stuff" framing, panic valve, no-shame copy, de-escalation, gentle defaults |
| App Store rejects blocking | Panic valve + user-control framing first, follow Family Controls guidelines, mirror Opal/Brick positioning |
| Incumbent copies the lock | Moat is the behavioral model + brand, not the lock alone |
| Stakes cause wellbeing harm | Hard caps, never-lock list, quiet-hours release, distress de-escalation, 13+ floor, no medical claims |
| Building a FlowSavvy-grade scheduler is hard | It is the bulk of P0 and must be done right; on-device engine + the detailed PRD algorithm make it tractable with AI coding |
| Solo founder, school schedule | Ship one slice at a time, use Claude Code as leverage, treat as a portfolio asset so even a slow build returns value |
| Distribution as an unknown solo dev | Lead with the one-sentence hook, seed ADHD/study creators, win one school first; virality is the plan, not paid ads |

## 10. Success metrics
Started-sooner time down over two weeks; staked tasks complete more than unstaked with panic-valve use under 15%; over half of users resume within two days of a lapse via Recovery; majority of Revealed Self suggestions accepted by week 3; stress trends down with no uninstall spike after the first stake; organic install rate from short-form content with at least one creator-driven spike.

## 11. Why now, why you
OS-level self-blocking (Apple Screen Time, Android) is mature and sanctioned; cheap personal LLMs make real behavioral parsing and grounded breakdowns feasible solo; ADHD/procrastination identity content is a massive sharing-heavy audience. The founder is inside the exact target audience, ships production apps solo with strong AI leverage, and has a shipping track record. Worst case, the artifact (a behavioral ADHD scheduler with OS-level app-locking on an open scheduling stack) is a strong engineering and college narrative piece. The downside is a portfolio win; the upside is a category.
