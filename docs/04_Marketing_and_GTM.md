# Ampora — Marketing & Go-To-Market

> The thesis: a student app wins on a story people retell in one sentence, plus a product that is actually helpful. Ampora has both. The job is to put the one-sentence hooks in front of the right feed and make the first session deliver the aha moment fast.

## 1. Positioning line
The scheduler that works with your brain instead of against it. It plans the real you, makes starting cost nothing, and makes slacking cost you your apps right now.

Keep that worldview as the soul (about page, brand voice). Sell the concrete features in the ads. Never lead with abstract phrases like "executive function operating system." Lead with the lock.

## 2. The hooks (each is a complete pitch with zero explanation)
- "An app that locks your Instagram until your homework is done." (headline)
- "It knows a 10-page paper doesn't actually take you an hour, and it fixes your schedule for you." (Revealed Self / time-blindness)
- "Hit overwhelmed and your whole calendar disappears except one step." (Blindfold)
- "Panic mode for the night before." (crunch)
- "You fell behind for three days and it just rebuilt your week. No guilt." (Recovery)

These five are the entire top of funnel. The lock is the lead.

## 3. Channels
- **Short-form video (primary):** TikTok, Reels, Shorts. Demo-first, real-screen clips. The lock is a 5-second "wait, it actually blocked my Instagram?" moment. Film it on a real phone.
- **ADHD / studytok creators:** seed a handful of mid-size ADHD and study creators. This audience evangelizes tools that feel built for them. FlowSavvy's own reviews are full of ADHD users; that demand is proven and underserved on the behavior side.
- **School-by-school ground game:** the founder's own school and clubs first. Density inside one school creates word of mouth and visible social proof, then it jumps schools.
- **Reddit / Discord:** r/ADHD, r/GetStudying, r/productivity, study Discords. Show, do not advertise.
- **The worldview as content:** "your brain isn't broken, it's built for intensity" is a banner students share. Brand voice is itself content.

## 4. The growth loop
1. A clip lands on the lock mechanic.
2. New user hits the aha fast: voice-dump or paste an assignment, get a real schedule, lock an app on the 2-minute starter action, start the thing they were avoiding.
3. They feel the difference (they started; no shame when they slip).
4. They post their own "this app locks my phone" clip or tell their group. Repeat.

The aha moment is the product's #1 marketing asset, so onboarding must reach "I locked Instagram and started the thing" in the first session.

## 5. First 10 content ideas
1. Screen-record locking Instagram, then starting a dreaded essay. Caption: "POV: your phone won't let you procrastinate."
2. The time-blindness callout: show it overriding "this takes 1 hour" with your real history.
3. Blindfold: tap overwhelmed, the calendar vanishes to one step.
4. Panic mode the night before a deadline.
5. Recovery: fall behind 3 days, one tap rebuilds the week.
6. "Apps I locked behind my homework this week" montage.
7. A day-in-the-life using the app as a student.
8. The worldview manifesto clip ("built for intensity, not grinding").
9. Voice brain-dump → full schedule in seconds.
10. Honest "FlowSavvy is great but I still procrastinated, so I built this."

## 6. Naming
Direction: a short, distinctive, ownable name in the energy/intensity lane (the founder liked "Adrenaline"), searchable enough that the app ranks for it.
- **Working pick: Ampora** (pushing an engine to its limit; intense; one word; brandable). Own "redline app" / "redline focus."
- Strong alternatives: **Lockin** (gen-z "lock in" = focus AND the app literally locks your apps; a coined one-word spelling helps SEO; the double meaning is marketing gold), **Throttle**, **Floored**, **Surge**, or a fully coined word for max ownability (e.g. **Adrenova**).
- **Searchability test before committing:** App Store name available, .com or a clean domain available, no dominant existing app/trademark on the exact term, and it ranks when you search it. Avoid common dictionary words already saturated. Coined or distinct-compound names rank fastest.

## 7. What not to do
- Do not lead with feature lists or the word "AI."
- Do not market the optional screen-aware monitoring as monitoring. If used, it is self-spying the user opted into, framed as a tool they point at themselves, seen only by them.
- Do not over-gamify. Streaks and loud rewards punish the exact audience. Calm and identity beat XP confetti.
- Do not promise to "cure" procrastination or make medical claims.

## 8. Launch sequence
1. Build to the aha (PRD P0 + P0.5): scheduler that feels great, voice/paste capture, iOS lock.
2. TestFlight with 10-50 real student testers from the founder's network; watch what they do, not what they say.
3. Cut the first lock-mechanic clip; post natively; seed 2-3 creators.
4. Iterate on the aha and onboarding based on where testers drop.
5. Public launch when the first session reliably reaches "I locked an app and started."
