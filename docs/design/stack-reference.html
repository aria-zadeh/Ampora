Ampora · Stack, refined

Same stack. It just stopped looking generated.

You picked Stack and you were right to. The card-stack skeleton, the segmented control plus FAB, the agenda-first calendar, none of that moves. What moves is everything a model reaches for by default when it hasn't made a real decision: the typeface, the gradient, the one radius used on everything, the one shadow used on everything, the flat two-weight type system. Below is what was actually wrong, then the fix, on the same four screens.

Why it read as AI
1
The typeface is Inter.

Every weight, every screen, one family: font-family:'Inter'. Inter is the default a generator reaches for because it is the one sans every model has seen the most captioned UI in. It is not wrong, it is just the tell that nobody chose anything. Fixed below.

2
The palette's one flourish is a stock gradient.

The Focus ring runs #60A5FA → #22D3EE → #2DD4BF, blue into cyan into mint. None of those three hues appears anywhere else in the app, they exist only to make one circle look expensive. A blue-cyan-teal glow behind a hero element is close to the single most common AI hero visual there is.

3
One radius, applied to everything.

border-radius:20px sits on the urgent strip, the focus card, every list row, every day card, every step row, at every size. A 44px row and a 235px hero card read as the same kind of object. Real hierarchy has a corner radius for a quiet row and a different one for the thing that matters.

4
One shadow, applied to almost everything.

Two shadow values exist in the whole file, and the stronger one is used exactly once. A resting list row and the screen's hero card cast the same lift. That is a component library's default elevation, not a designed surface.

5
No rest, no density.

Every gap in every stack is the same 8px, from one list row to the next and from the urgent strip into the hero card into the section header. Nothing tells your eye "new paragraph." A designed screen has a place that's tight and a place that breathes.

6
Type varies in size only.

Grep the file: every rule is weight 600 or 700, nothing lighter, nowhere. No regular weight anywhere in the whole screen. Real type systems modulate weight and let most of the screen sit at regular, reserving heavier weight for the few things that have earned it.

7
Icons are one register: thin outline, everywhere.

Every glyph is the same 1.8–2.2px stroke outline regardless of what it's doing. Real, worth naming, but out of scope for a surgical pass, noted here rather than touched below.

Kept, and changed

Kept exactly

  • The vertical stack of equal-width cards, focus card simply taller
  • Top segmented control plus a single circular FAB
  • Agenda-first calendar, no grid
  • One primary action per screen, the calm overall pace
  • Blue as the one accent. It's Ampora's real primary, not a generic pick
  • Every screen's content, copy, and category colors (teal Math, amber Spanish) exactly as specced

Changed, surgically

  • Typeface: Inter → Lexend (see note below)
  • Gradient orb → a single-hue progress ring, one blue tonal step, functional not decorative
  • One radius → three: 12 quiet rows, 18 the Today feature card, 26 the Focus hero
  • One shadow → four tiers, warm-tinted, matched to what's actually elevated
  • Flat 8px rhythm → tight within a group, a real 18px break between groups
  • Two weights → four (400/500/600/700), regular carries most of the screen now
  • Cool near-white canvas → warm bone canvas and ink, Ampora's own current palette
On the typeface, specifically

The pick is Lexend (@expo-google-fonts/lexend). It shows up in the font-pairing set for exactly this brief (calm, accessible, corporate-trustworthy) and it has a better reason than looking nice: Lexend was engineered with reading researchers and validated in a Stanford study for reading speed and reduced visual stress. For an app built for people who procrastinate and people with ADHD, that is not a vibe, it is the actual point.

It is installed: node_modules/@expo-google-fonts/lexend/ in this repo, four weights (400/500/600/700), the same package the app itself loads via @expo-google-fonts/lexend.

The four screens below render in the real thing, not a stand-in: those four TTFs subset to exactly the characters this page uses, converted to woff2, base64'd, self-contained, font-family:'Lexend' throughout. One accent, not a ramp. No purple anywhere, no second decorative hue. Warm Stone neutrals replace the cooler Zinc-adjacent grays Stack shipped with, which is also where Ampora's own design system has already moved (docs/02 §14). The gradient is gone; the only place blue varies is one tonal step, deep to primary, on the progress ring, because it's showing elapsed time, not standing in for taste.
