# Viral productivity, focus, screen-time, study, ADHD, habit and self-improvement apps (2026-08-14 to 2026-10-14)

Method note: about 40 web searches. WebFetch failed with DNS errors on every page tried (abcnews.com, aiweekly.co, getappniche.com, appcurrents.com), and the proxy refused appfigures.com with a policy 403. So the figures below come from search-result snippets of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Appfigures, Sensor Tower and RevenueCat category reports for this exact window could not be read directly. **Overall the evidence for this exact window is thin.** Only one app in scope (Gauth) has both a user metric and a revenue metric dated inside the window, and both are weak (a cumulative store install count plus a third-party revenue estimate). Nothing here is fabricated. Where a number is older than the window, it is labelled as context.

## Q1. Which focus/app-blocking or screen-time apps gained the most downloads or revenue in Sept/Oct 2026, and what was their hook?

### Takeaway
I found no app-blocker or screen-time app with a verifiable download or revenue spike dated 2026-08-14 to 2026-10-14. The in-window signals are retail and press only: Brick's back-to-school sale coverage and its Amazon "bought in past month" badge. Opal shipped routine updates in the window. The Productivity chart in the window belonged to Meta's AI agent app Muse, not to any focus app.

### Cited Findings
**Brick (hardware NFC puck plus app, Brick)**
- ABC News/GMA ran "I tried the viral Brick app-blocker for 2 weeks. It's now on sale for back-to-school" (late August 2026, per search snippet) — [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Shop/brick-review/story?id=134547488)
- Amazon listing shows "9K+ bought in past month". This is a retailer badge, not audited sales, and the month it refers to is not dated — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Brick-Phone-Blocker-Device-Lock-Screen/dp/B0GQ6VV79M)
- Price is $59, one-time, no subscription. A third-party blog lists a 10% code and 20% off for students — [whatifididnt.com](https://whatifididnt.com/blog/brick-phone-app/); [TechCrunch review, 2026-06-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/if-you-want-to-cut-your-screen-time-just-get-a-brick/)
- Context from before the window: Fortune (2026-02-13) reported Gen Z buying "$40 app blockers" (Bloom, Brick) to curb the "slot machine in your pocket" — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/13/analog-gen-z-phone-addiction-bloom-brick-app-blockers-dumb-phones-social-media/)
- I found no company-reported unit sales or revenue for Brick (search for founder plus units sold returned nothing relevant) — [PitchBook search result](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/513261-01)

**Opal (Opal: The Focus Company)**
- The Google Play listing shows developer updates dated 24 Sept 2026 and 7 Oct 2026. App Store release notes for 4.11 and 4.12 cover more detected distracting apps and more reliable background blocking, and name "Family and School" features as future work. No news coverage in the window — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.withopal.opal&hl=en_US); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/opal-screen-time-control/id1497465230)

**Platform context**
- Apple and the AAP released a family digital-habits guide on 2026-09-29 — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-releases-new-guide-to-help-families-build-healthy-digital-habits/)
- A vendor blog says iOS 27 added category-level "Time Allowances" aimed at parents, while adults keep the "Ignore Limit" escape hatch, so third-party blockers remain necessary for self-imposed limits. This is a single source with a conflict of interest, and Apple has not confirmed it — [Habit Doom](https://habitdoom.com/blog/ios-27-screen-time-changes)

**Productivity chart in the window (not a focus app)**
- Meta's Muse AI agent app hit #1 overall on the US App Store on 2026-09-18 and was #1 in US Productivity in early October. A Sensor Tower estimate puts it past 5M cumulative US downloads within 22 days. Meta has disclosed no active users or revenue — [TechCrunch 2026-09-21](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/metas-muse-is-outpacing-chatgpts-early-mobile-launch/); [TechCrunch 2026-09-25](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/); [9to5Mac 2026-10-07](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/07/meta-launches-muse-for-ipad-following-weeks-as-the-top-iphone-app/); [Tokenpost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/27373)

### Inferences
- In this window, AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Muse) crowded the Productivity chart and its grossing ranks. No focus or blocker app broke through at the chart level.
- In the press, the window's screen-time story is hardware (Brick) plus back-to-school discounts, not a new app.
- The hooks that recur (Brick, Bloom, 67, Opal "hard mode") are all about friction an adult cannot easily undo. That fits the reported iOS 27 gap.

### Gaps
- No Appfigures or Sensor Tower Sept/Oct 2026 download or revenue data for Opal, one sec, Brick, Forest, AppBlock or ScreenZen could be read (appfigures.com was blocked by the proxy, and the pages that came up had no figures for this window).
- No company-reported Brick unit sales.
- No one sec data for the window. Its pricing ($2.99/mo, $19.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime) comes only from a competitor's blog — [unhookd.app](https://unhookd.app/blog/best-screen-time-apps-for-adults)

## Q2. Which study/homework apps rode back-to-school 2026 to the top of the Education or Productivity charts, with what revenue?

### Takeaway
AI homework and study apps were the back-to-school story. Gauth (ByteDance), Gizmo and Turbo AI all climbed the US Education chart together in September 2026. **Gauth is the only app in this whole brief with both a user metric and a revenue metric dated inside the window**, and the revenue figure is a third-party estimate.

### Cited Findings
**Apps with BOTH a user metric and a revenue metric in the window**
- **Gauth (ByteDance)**
  - Chart: reached #3 overall in US Education (+2), per an AI Weekly piece dated 2026-09-21 — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/editors-blog/in-the-wild-2026-09-21)
  - Users, company-reported: an 2026-08-31 press release launching AI math courses (200+ courses plus user-generated courses) says "surpassing 200 million downloads" — [K12 Dive press release](https://www.k12dive.com/press-release/20260831-all-in-one-study-platform-gauth-launches-user-generated-education/)
  - Users, store or third-party: Forbes (2026-08-31) and others say 100M+ on Google Play. The gap with 200M likely reflects Android only versus all platforms, but no source says so — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ianshepherd/2026/08/31/gauth-uses-ai-to-let-every-student-create-a-personalized-course/)
  - Revenue, third-party estimate: about $4M/month on iOS, #2 Education grosser for September 2026 behind Duolingo (~$6M/mo), ahead of Quizlet (~$2M/mo) — [getappniche](https://getappniche.com/top-grossing-ios-apps/education)
  - Pricing: about $9.99–$11.99/month freemium. One July 2026 analysis says Gauth moved to $11.99/mo subscriptions "as ByteDance seeks revenue after years of subsidized growth" — [implicator.ai](https://www.implicator.ai/bytedances-homework-app-gauthmath-quietly-conquers-american-classrooms/); [myengineeringbuddy](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/gauth-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings-in-2026/)
  - Audience: US K-12 and college STEM students. Some US schools block it on their networks — [Gauth review](https://ucstrategies.com/news/gauth-ai-review-can-this-tool-really-help-you-study-like-a-real-teacher/)

**Apps with ONLY ONE metric (labelled section)**
- **Gizmo: AI Tutor (Gizmo, Cambridge founders)**
  - Chart: rose to #10 in US Education (+4) in September 2026 — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/editors-blog/in-the-wild-2026-09-21)
  - Users, company-reported but before the window: 13M learners at its $22M Series A (2026-04-15, led by Shine Capital) — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/gizmo-raises-22-million-series-a-to-make-learning-addictive-for-13-million-learners-worldwide-302741943.html)
  - Growth channel: word of mouth. Positioned as "studying as addictive as TikTok" — [TFN](https://techfundingnews.com/gizmo-22m-series-a-ai-learning-app/)
  - Pricing: free tier with daily limits, about $155/yr (third-party) — [aiera.blog](https://aiera.blog/gizmo-ai-review-2026-is-this-study-app-worth-it/)
  - Revenue: none found.
- **Turbo AI (TurboLearn Inc.)**
  - Chart: up 5 spots in Education in the same September window — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/editors-blog/in-the-wild-2026-09-21)
  - Users and revenue, both founder-reported and both from October 2025, before the window: 5M users and "eight-figure ARR", about $20/mo — [Yahoo/TechCrunch](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/20-old-dropouts-built-ai-192500155.html)
  - No in-window update found.

### Gaps

## Q3. Which ADHD-targeted apps grew in this window, and how did they position themselves?

### Takeaway
I found no ADHD app with a dated launch, funding round or growth spike between 2026-08-14 and 2026-10-14. The available data is pricing, ratings and ownership news from earlier in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Inflow** was acquired by Cerebral in March 2026 and now lists $47.99/mo or $199/yr with a 7-day trial. A critic disputes the RCT claim made at the acquisition — [TilTaken](https://www.tiltaken.com/articles/inflow-cerebral-acquisition)
- **Tiimo** was Apple's 2025 iPhone App of the Year, with a 4.59 rating from 20K+ US ratings and about $42–$54/yr. Its last funding I found was €1.4M in August 2024 — [soothfy](https://soothfy.com/blog/tiimo-alternatives.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiimo)
- **Finch** has very large rating counts (~757K to ~1.38M), but it positions itself as self-care rather than ADHD — [habitbox](https://habitbox.app/blog/finch-app-review)
- Market reports put the 2026 ADHD app market at about $2.0B–$2.8B. The vendor methodologies differ several-fold — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/adhd-apps-market-report)

### Inferences
- In 2026, ADHD apps position themselves as clinical-adjacent (Inflow, now under Cerebral) or visual-planner (Tiimo). Neither showed a back-to-school breakout in the sources found.

### Gaps
- No user or revenue metric for any ADHD app inside the window. I did not reach r/ADHD sentiment for the window.

## Q4. What pricing and paywall patterns did the top earners in these categories use?

### Takeaway
Patterns across the category: a generous free tier to drive daily use (Opal), weekly or monthly plans for goal-driven Education apps, one-time hardware pricing ($39–$79) as a subscription-free counter-position, and freemium AI study tools at $10–20/month.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat 2026: monthly plans are 77% of Productivity revenue. Education skews annual (59–66%) but renews poorly on annual plans and does well on weekly ones. Day-0 conversion is 71.9% for Productivity and 28.5% for Education — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [RevenueCat renewals](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/average-subscription-renewal-rates-by-app-category)
- Trial length: Education favours 5–9-day trials. Productivity favours 4 days or less — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/free-trial-length)
- Education top 10% of apps take 96% of the category's subscription revenue — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/education-app-subscription-benchmarks/)
- Opal: deliberately lowered paid conversion from 20% to 9% to grow DAU and revenue — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/kenneth-schlenker-sub-club-podcast-2026)
- Hardware blockers: Brick $59 one-time, Bloom about $39–$47, Unpluq about $59–$79 plus subscription, Detach $9.99 — [unhookd](https://unhookd.app/blog/best-physical-phone-blockers-2026); [whatifididnt](https://whatifididnt.com/blog/physical-phone-blocker/)
- Looksmaxxing: Umax at $3.99/week. Older founder claims of about $500K/month, and a 2026 roundup estimate of ~$6M/yr. None of this is in the window — [Yahoo/Fortune](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942148.html); [HyScaler](https://hyscaler.com/insights/looksmax-ai-self-improvement-app-men/)

### Inferences
- A weekly paywall is the looksmaxxing and AI-tool norm. Focus apps lean on a free core plus a monthly or lifetime plan. Hardware sells "no subscription" as the hook.

### Gaps
- No in-window revenue data for any looksmaxxing app. The only in-window looksmaxxing news was legal and safety coverage (for example, the 2026-10-13 Clavicular lawsuit update), not app metrics — [Boston 25](https://www.boston25news.com/news/local/influencer-claviculars-accuser-drops-sexual-assault-charges-civil-lawsuit-amid-case-mass/GWWYCWVNLFE4XJRCISG6WPWP7A/)
- No habit-tracker breakout was found. Blossom (formerly Fern), Tinyville and Fabulous recur only in TikTok creator posts, with no metrics — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/habit-tracker-apps)
