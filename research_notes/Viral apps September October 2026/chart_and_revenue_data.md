# New and breakout consumer apps, 2026-08-14 to 2026-10-14: chart and revenue data

Research date: 2026-10-14.

**Method caveat, read first.** Direct page fetches failed in this session (DNS errors on appfigures.com and techcrunch.com), so every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from reading the full article text. Numbers and dates match across several independent outlets where noted, but the report writer should treat single-source figures as "reported, not re-verified against the primary page". Appfigures' own September and October 2026 posts were only partly indexed. The September 2026 monthly "most downloaded / top earning" insight (normally published in early October) did not turn up in any search, and no October 2026 "This Week in Apps" edition was indexed.

**Bottom line.** The window is thin. Only one consumer app launched inside the window AND has both a cited user metric and a cited revenue metric: **Meta's Muse** (launched 2026-09-08). One more, **xAI's Grok Bot**, has both metrics but launched 2026-08-11, three days before the window opens, so it is borderline. No other new or breakout consumer app with hard download plus revenue evidence surfaced for this window. I did not pad the list with older breakouts (UpScrolled, Freecash, short-drama apps, Meta AI's April climb), which are listed as excluded below.

## Which apps appeared in Appfigures' weekly/monthly posts (biggest movers, top new apps, top earners) for September and October 2026?

### Takeaway
Appfigures' September 2026 coverage was dominated by one new app, Meta's Muse, which it reported knocking ChatGPT from #1 with about 1.1M downloads in 11 days while earning only about $27K net. The other September editions covered evergreen apps (ChatGPT, TikTok, WhatsApp, Vinted, X, mobile games), and no October 2026 edition was indexed.

### Cited Findings
- Appfigures insight "Meta's New AI Worker Just Knocked ChatGPT From #1" reports Muse reached #1 on both the App Store and Google Play with about 1.1M downloads in its first 11 days, downloads almost entirely in the US and mostly via the App Store. Publication date not shown in the extract, it is featured in the 2026-09-18 This Week in Apps edition. ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/meta-finally-gets-serious-about-ai-muse-million-downloads))
- The same Appfigures analysis estimates Muse earned about $27K after store fees in its first 11 days, versus about $175K for xAI's Grok Bot over its own first 11 days. Third-party estimate. ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/meta-finally-gets-serious-about-ai-muse-million-downloads), repeated by [Business Model Analyst](https://businessmodelanalyst.com/meta-muse-downloads-revenue-per-download/))
- Appfigures argues Meta can drive downloads cheaply through its own ad platform and that the installs may reflect "curiosity" rather than real demand. Appfigures CEO Ariel Michaeli is quoted making the curiosity point. ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/meta-finally-gets-serious-about-ai-muse-million-downloads), [Business Model Analyst](https://businessmodelanalyst.com/meta-muse-downloads-revenue-per-download/))
- This Week in Apps #269, dated 2026-09-18, titled "Knocking ChatGPT Down", covered Meta's AI worker overtaking ChatGPT at #1, August's most downloaded and highest-earning apps, Vinted's US push, and X's revenue record under Grok. This Week in Apps #270, dated 2026-09-25, titled "Traditional Streamers Beware", covered mobile games and short dramas. Titles and dates come from the Appfigures listing page as indexed, full text not retrieved. ([Appfigures This Week in Apps index](https://appfigures.com/resources/this-week-in-apps))
- Appfigures' August 2026 worldwide download ranking, posted on X (reported as 2026-09-15): ChatGPT 53M, TikTok 40M, WhatsApp 38M, estimated. All evergreen, no new entrant in the top three. ([Appfigures on X](https://x.com/appfigures/status/2099875233486213501))
- Appfigures (via TechCrunch, 2026-09-25, Sarah Perez) put Muse installs at roughly 2.3M "as of Thursday" (2026-09-24), almost evenly split between iOS and Android. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/))
- TechCrunch, 2026-09-21: Muse logged more downloads and DAUs in the US and Canada than ChatGPT did over the same post-launch period, citing Appfigures, with a matched US+Canada iOS subset of 1.8M Muse downloads versus 1.3M for ChatGPT over 12 days. The 2.8M global install figure in that piece is attributed to Apptopia in one extract and to Appfigures in another. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/metas-muse-is-outpacing-chatgpts-early-mobile-launch/), [Yahoo Tech summary](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/article/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-chasing-chatgpts-app-store-rise--and-hit-no-1-with-fewer-downloads-152809095.html))

### Inferences
- Appfigures' own framing is the most useful analytical point for the report: Muse is a distribution-driven chart topper with weak early monetization (about 2.5 cents net per install), not a demand-driven viral hit.
- Vinted appears in Appfigures' September coverage, but its US surge started in early 2026 (see Q3), so it is a continuation, not a new breakout in this window.

### Gaps
- Appfigures' September 2026 monthly "most downloaded" and "top earning" insights (normally early October) were not found. If they exist, they would be the best source for any other new entrant.
- No October 2026 This Week in Apps edition was indexed.
- Full text of #269 and #270 could not be read, so any smaller "biggest movers" or "top new apps" items inside them are unknown.

## Which apps did Sensor Tower or AppMagic flag as breakout or fastest-growing new apps in September/October 2026?

### Takeaway
Sensor Tower's only identified breakout call in the window is again Muse, which it tracked from about 730K US downloads by day 10 to more than 5M within 22 days, the fastest of any major AI assistant. AppMagic's September 2026 data covers games only, and the top grossers are all evergreen titles.

### Cited Findings
- Sensor Tower: Muse hit #1 on the US App Store on 2026-09-18, ten days after launch, with more than 730K US downloads over that stretch. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/meta-muse-overtakes-chatgpt-1-103903374.html), [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/article/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-chasing-chatgpts-app-store-rise--and-hit-no-1-with-fewer-downloads-152809095.html))
- Sensor Tower via CNBC, 2026-09-21: Muse logged over 2.5M downloads in its first 13 days, ahead of Claude (400K) and Grok (200K) over the same post-launch window. Secondary outlets cite about 3.1M for ChatGPT's first 13 days. Sensor Tower cautioned that staggered iOS and Android launches distort the comparison. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-downloads.html), [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/09/meta-muse-ai-outpaces-chatgpt-early-downloads.html))
- Sensor Tower: more than 3.4M downloads by about 2026-09-25. Apptopia: 4.3M (2.6M on iOS). Appfigures: about 2.3M. ([Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/meta-s-muse-tops-3-4-million-estimated-downloads-as-its-promotion-ramps-up), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/))
- Sensor Tower: Muse passed 5M downloads in 22 days, versus 56 days for ChatGPT, 103 days for Grok and 492 days for Claude. Reported 2026-10-01. One extract (Forbes-sourced) says this is US-only, others do not specify. Treat the geography as unconfirmed. ([Tech Startups, 2026-10-01](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/01/metas-muse-ai-hits-5-million-downloads-in-22-days-outpacing-chatgpt-grok-and-claude/), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-muse-5-million-downloads-22-days/))
- A 6.6M cumulative Muse download figure circulates but is attributed to Citi by one source and to Sensor Tower by another, so it is unconfirmed. ([The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/metas-muse-hit-5-million-downloads-the-bigger-story-is-how-it-got-there/))
- Muse estimated DAU around 700K by late September, i.e. most installers were not daily users. Underlying firm not clear in the extract. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-muse-app-store-retention/))
- TickerTrends data (reported by Hunterbrook Media, which discloses a short position in Meta): Muse daily downloads about 170K on 2026-10-06, down 21.1% from a peak of about 215K on 2026-09-24. Treat as a biased source. ([Hunterbrook](https://hntrbrk.com/breaking-news/muse-tickertrends))
- Bloomberg Intelligence estimated Muse had been downloaded four times as often as Grok Bot, and Grok Bot passed 400K weekly users about a month after launch (Bloomberg-sourced, via PYMNTS). ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/spacexai-grok-bot-gains-early-traction-ai-agent-push/))
- AppMagic September 2026 games (published by Mellow/GameDev Reports, about 2026-10-05): Honor of Kings $135M (-12% MoM), Gossip Harbor $99.1M, Royal Match $91.3M, MONOPOLY GO! $81.5M, Pokemon GO $61.9M (+23% MoM), PUBG MOBILE $55.2M (-50%). All evergreen, no new title in the top ranks. ([GameDev Reports / AppMagic](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-2a7))
- Sensor Tower's most recent short-drama breakout list (FreeReels, HotMiniDrama, LuckyDrama, BonusTV, iDrama, NetShort) is Q1 2026, outside the window. ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report))

### Inferences
- The three firms disagree by roughly 2x on Muse downloads at the same point in time (Appfigures 2.3M, Sensor Tower 3.4M, Apptopia 4.3M around 2026-09-24/25). The report should present a range, not a single number.
- Appfigures' own Muse series looks internally inconsistent (2.8M global cited at day 12, 2.3M cited later). This may be an attribution mix-up with Apptopia rather than an Appfigures revision.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower or AppMagic "fastest-growing new apps" list for September or October 2026 was found. Sensor Tower's Q3 2026 report was not indexed.
- No AppMagic data on new non-game apps for the window.

## Which new apps hit #1 overall or #1 in a category on the US App Store or Google Play in this window, and for how long?

### Takeaway
Muse is the only new app confirmed at #1 overall: #1 US App Store free from 2026-09-18, #1 Google Play from 2026-09-19, and still #1 US free in a 2026-10-04 snapshot, i.e. at least about 16 days and still running at last check. No other new app reaching #1 overall or #1 in a category in the window was found.

### Cited Findings
- Muse climbed to the top of the US App Store on Friday 2026-09-18 and had held it through the article date. It reached #1 on Google Play on 2026-09-19 and remained there. TechCrunch, 2026-09-25. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/))
- Muse launched 2026-09-08 for US users aged 18+. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-muse-app-store-retention/), [CNBC 2026-09-08](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/meta-personal-ai-agents-public-reckoning-privacy-safety.html))
- Third-party chart snapshot dated 2026-10-04, US top free: #1 Muse from Meta, #2 Vinted, #3 ChatGPT. A live Appfigures US iPhone chart showed the same top three when indexed. ([AppStoreStatistics top free](https://appstorestatistics.com/charts/us/top-free), [Appfigures live chart](https://app.appfigures.com/top-apps/ios-app-store/united-states/iphone/top-overall))
- US top grossing over the same period was held by ChatGPT, on a 22-day run at #1 as of 2026-10-06, with YouTube and TikTok next as of 2026-10-12. No new app in the top grossing ranks. ([AppStoreStatistics top grossing](https://appstorestatistics.com/charts/us/top-grossing), [Applyra](https://www.applyra.io/tools/top-charts/app-store/us/overall/grossing))
- A third-party revenue tool dated 2026-10-11 ranked Muse sixth by estimated monthly downloads. ([GetAppNiche](https://getappniche.com/top-grossing-ios-apps))
- Grok Bot: no App Store or Google Play chart position found. Google Play shows 1M+ installs (a range floor), listing updated 2026-10-10. ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.x.grok.bot&hl=en_US))

### Inferences
- Muse's #1 run (2026-09-18 through at least 2026-10-04) coincides with heavy Meta cross-promotion, which TechCrunch's headline describes as Meta "putting its muscle behind Muse". This supports Appfigures' view that distribution, not organic virality, drove the chart position.
- Vinted at #2 US top free is notable but is a European incumbent whose US growth started in early 2026 (Appfigures: 2.6M US downloads Jan-Apr 2026 vs about 286K a year earlier), so it does not meet the "launched or broke out in window" test. ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/vinted-comes-to-the-us))

### Gaps
- Category #1s for new apps in the window (e.g. Lifestyle, Sports, Photo & Video) could not be established. One rank tracker listed Hark Pro (released 2026-09-30) as rising in US Lifestyle and Tailgate: Call the Shots at #19 in US Sports on 2026-10-07, but neither reached #1 and neither has download or revenue figures. ([App Store Trends](https://appstoretrends.xyz/blog/2026-10-07-top-day))
- Exact end date of Muse's #1 run is unknown (no snapshot after 2026-10-04 confirmed its rank).

## What revenue (monthly gross, first-month revenue) is estimated for each?

### Takeaway
Only two apps have a cited revenue figure, both from a single Appfigures analysis covering each app's first 11 days: Muse about $27K net and Grok Bot about $175K net. No monthly gross or full first-month figure was found for either, and neither company has reported in-app revenue.

### Cited Findings

#### Qualifying list (user metric AND revenue metric, launch in window)

**1. Muse (Meta)**
- Developer: Meta Platforms, Inc.
- Category: AI personal agent / assistant (exact store category not confirmed)
- Launch: 2026-09-08, US, 18+. Breakout: #1 US App Store free on 2026-09-18, #1 Google Play on 2026-09-19. ([TechCrunch 2026-09-25](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/))
- What it does: an always-on personal AI agent from Meta that acts on the user's behalf, built on Meta's Muse Spark model family. ([CNBC 2026-09-08](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/meta-personal-ai-agents-public-reckoning-privacy-safety.html))
- Downloads (all third-party estimates): Appfigures about 1.1M in first 11 days ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/meta-finally-gets-serious-about-ai-muse-million-downloads)), about 2.3M by 2026-09-24 ([TechCrunch 2026-09-25](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/)). Sensor Tower 730K US by 2026-09-18, 2.5M by 2026-09-21 ([CNBC 2026-09-21](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-downloads.html)), 3.4M by about 2026-09-25 ([Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/meta-s-muse-tops-3-4-million-estimated-downloads-as-its-promotion-ramps-up)), 5M+ in 22 days ([Tech Startups 2026-10-01](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/01/metas-muse-ai-hits-5-million-downloads-in-22-days-outpacing-chatgpt-grok-and-claude/)). Apptopia 4.3M (2.6M iOS) ([TechCrunch 2026-09-25](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/)).
- Usage: about 700K estimated DAU in late September ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-muse-app-store-retention/)). Meta has not released adoption figures ([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/article/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-chasing-chatgpts-app-store-rise--and-hit-no-1-with-fewer-downloads-152809095.html)).
- Revenue: about $27K after store fees in first 11 days, about 2.5 cents per install on about 1.1M downloads. Appfigures third-party estimate, store revenue only. Muse also sells via web and WhatsApp, which bypass Apple and Google, so true revenue is higher than this. ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/meta-finally-gets-serious-about-ai-muse-million-downloads), [Business Model Analyst](https://businessmodelanalyst.com/meta-muse-downloads-revenue-per-download/))
- Monetization: freemium subscription. Free tier, Power $20/month (about 500M tokens/week), Maximum $100/month (about 3B tokens/week), monthly billing only per guides. Sources are third-party guides and Toolradar says there is no official public pricing page, so treat prices as reported, not confirmed by Meta. ([Usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/meta-muse-pricing/), [Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/meta-muse-pricing), [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/meta-muse/pricing))
- Growth drivers: Meta's own distribution and ad platform plus cross-promotion across its apps, press coverage of "beating ChatGPT's launch". Appfigures attributes installs to curiosity driven by Meta's reach rather than organic demand. ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/meta-finally-gets-serious-about-ai-muse-million-downloads), [TechCrunch 2026-09-25](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/)). No TikTok or influencer driver found. No App Store editorial feature found.

### Inferences
- Muse's revenue per install (about $0.025 net) is roughly an order of magnitude below Grok Bot's on a similar window, consistent with Grok Bot launching to an existing paid subscriber base while Muse launched free to a mass audience.
- Muse's $27K covers only store billing in days 1 to 11 and predates its #1 run, so it likely understates September revenue materially. No later estimate exists to confirm this.

### Gaps
- No monthly gross or first full month revenue for Muse or Grok Bot from Appfigures, Sensor Tower, AppMagic or data.ai.
- No company-reported revenue or subscriber numbers from Meta or xAI for these products.
- No new consumer app outside the AI-agent category with both download and revenue evidence was found for 2026-08-14 to 2026-10-14. This may reflect thin indexing of very recent Appfigures and Sensor Tower posts rather than a true absence. A follow-up once Appfigures' September 2026 monthly insights are indexed would close this.
- Meta's Muse Wonder model launch (around 2026-10-12/13) may have driven a second Muse spike, but no post-launch download or revenue data was found. ([Quartz](https://qz.com/meta-stock-muse-wonder-ai-model-launch-101326))
